Will I see a quote before work begins?+

Yes. Every repair starts with an assessment, and you review the proposed repair, part options and confirmed quote before deciding.

Are the prices on this site final?+

No. They are illustrative starting prices. Your model, diagnosis, part selection and availability determine the final quote.

Should I back up my phone first?+

Where possible, yes — especially for software issues, where a reset may be considered.

Can you check if an accessory fits my phone?+

Yes. Choose an accessory and prepare an enquiry with your exact model to check compatibility and availability.

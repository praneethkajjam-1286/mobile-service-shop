What shapes your quote

Four things
set the final price.

01

Your model

Your phone model determines which parts fit, their availability and the time needed to complete the repair.

02

The diagnosis

A careful assessment identifies the underlying fault, which can differ from the first symptom you noticed.

03

Part selection

We explain compatible part options, their quality and price so you can choose before repair work begins.

04

Availability

Parts availability for your exact model can affect the final price and how soon your repair is ready.

Pricing questions

Straight answers.

Are these prices final?

No. They are illustrative starting ranges. The final quote is confirmed after your device is assessed.

Do I pay for the assessment?

Ask the studio when you prepare your enquiry, so you know before you visit.

Why is software the same for all ranges?

Software support doesn't usually involve replacement parts, so the starting range doesn't change by device range.

What if the fault is different from what I expected?

We'll explain what we found and give you a new confirmed quote. Nothing proceeds without your decision.

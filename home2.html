Make it last

Small habits.
Longer life.

Simple care tips that help a repaired phone stay healthy. Flip through the deck.

1 / 5

Use a case and screen glass

A well-fitted case and protector are the simplest way to avoid the next crack.

Charge with the right gear

Use a cable and adapter suited to your phone's charging format.

Avoid extreme heat

Leaving your phone in hot cars or direct sun can wear the battery faster.

Keep software updated

Updates often include fixes. Back up first, then update when you can.

Keep the port clear

Pocket lint can block charging. Avoid metal objects; ask us to check it.

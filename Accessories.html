Charging, explained

Know your format.

C

USB-C

A reversible connector used by most current Android phones and newer iPhones. One cable often charges several devices.

Most common today
L

Lightning

The connector on many older iPhones. If your phone uses it, check your cable's connector before buying.

Older iPhones
≋

Wireless

Charging pads work only with phones that support wireless charging. Thick cases can affect it.
